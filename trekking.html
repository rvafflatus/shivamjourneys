Handpicked Himalayan & Alpine Expeditions

10 Destinations
Ladakh Region
Extreme • 9 Days Best Time: Jan - Feb

1. Chadar Trek, Ladakh

Walk over the frozen Zanskar River amidst dramatic canyon walls in sub-zero temperatures. A once-in-a-lifetime extreme winter adventure for seasoned trekkers.

Himachal Pradesh
Moderate • 5 Days Best Time: June - Oct

2. Hampta Pass Trek, Himachal Pradesh

A dramatic crossover trail connecting the lush green Kullu valley to the stark, arid landscapes of Lahaul & Spiti. Ideal for enthusiastic beginners.

Jammu & Kashmir
Difficult • 7 Days Best Time: July - Sept

3. Kashmir Great Lakes Trek, Jammu & Kashmir

Widely regarded as heaven on earth, this trail takes you past vibrant meadows, pine forests, and breathtaking turquoise alpine lakes.

Uttarakhand
Easy-Moderate • 5 Days Best Time: July - Aug

4. Valley of Flowers Trek, Uttarakhand

A vibrant UNESCO World Heritage Site blanketed by hundreds of species of endemic alpine wildflowers during the monsoon season.

Uttarakhand
Difficult • 8 Days Best Time: May - Oct

5. Roopkund Trek, Uttarakhand

Famous for the mysterious skeletal remains found at the edge of the glacial lake, offering magnificent views of Mount Trishul and Nanda Ghunti.

West Bengal
Moderate • 6 Days Best Time: Oct - April

6. Sandakphu Trek, West Bengal

The highest point in West Bengal providing a rare panoramic 'Sleeping Buddha' view encompassing four of the world's five highest peaks.

Uttarakhand
Moderate • 7 Days Best Time: Mar - Jun, Sep - Dec

7. Har Ki Dun Trek, Uttarakhand

A cradle-shaped hanging valley rich in mythology, ancient wooden villages, alpine forests, and stunning glacier views.

Uttarakhand
Difficult • 7 Days Best Time: May - Jun, Sep - Oct

8. Kedartal Trek, Uttarakhand

A pristine glacial lake situated at 4,750 meters near the majestic Thalaysagar peak. A challenging high-altitude trek for experienced trekkers.

Jammu & Kashmir
Moderate-Difficult • 7 Days Best Time: July - Sep

9. Tarsar Marsar Trek, Jammu & Kashmir

Explore almond-shaped twin alpine lakes hidden in the folds of the Kashmir mountains, surrounded by breathtaking green meadows.

Kerala
Moderate • 2 Days Best Time: Sep - Feb

10. Meesapulimala Trek, Kerala

The second highest peak in the Western Ghats offering a surreal experience of walking above the clouds through rolling shola forests and tea estates.
