Curated Himalayan & Alpine Trails

Moderate • 4-5 Days Best Time: June - Oct

1. Hampta Pass Trek, Himachal Pradesh

A dramatic crossover trek connecting the lush green Kullu valley to the stark, rugged landscape of Lahaul & Spiti. Perfect for enthusiastic beginners and nature lovers.

Difficult • 7-8 Days Best Time: July - Sept

2. Kashmir Great Lakes Trek, Jammu & Kashmir

Widely considered one of the most pristine alpine lake treks in the world. Walk past vibrant meadows, pine forests, and turquoise high-altitude lakes.

Easy to Moderate • 5 Days Best Time: July - Aug

3. Valley of Flowers Trek, Uttarakhand

A UNESCO World Heritage site blanketed by hundreds of species of endemic alpine flowers during the monsoon season. A visual treat for photographers and family trekkers.

Essential Trek Guidelines & Safety

  • Cardio Fitness: Start running or brisk walking at least 3 weeks prior to high-altitude treks.
  • Hydration: Drink 3-4 liters of water daily during the trek to prevent AMS (Acute Mountain Sickness).
  • Layering: Pack thermal innerwear, windproof jackets, and waterproof trekking shoes.
  • Certified Support: All trips include experienced guides, medical kits, and safety gear.
